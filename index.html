Reading promoters from DNA shape

Research with Dr. Shibasish Chowdhury, BITS Pilani

Promoters are the stretches of DNA where a gene switches on. Most models read them as text. Mine also reads their three-dimensional shape.

Each 81-base window is seen three ways: DNA-BERT-2 sequence embeddings, 14 deepDNAshape structural features per base, and a graph attention network that treats every base as a node.

Reading shape as a graph beats reading it as a flat list, 0.86 to 0.80.

Jan – May 2026

Floods, seen through cloud

Transformer change detection on Sentinel-1 radar

Monsoon floods arrive under cloud cover, which blinds optical satellites. Radar sees straight through it. I trained transformer change-detection models on 10,255 before-and-after Sentinel-1 tiles from the 2018 Hardoi floods.

0.945mIoU on 1,539 held-out tiles, with flood pixels outnumbered 34 to 1. A Swin-UNet hybrid beat ChangeFormer's 0.892 and converged three times faster.

A tutor that learns by teaching

Research with Dr. Jagat Sesh Challa, BITS Pilani

People learn a subject best by teaching it. This project applies the same idea to language models, fine-tuning them in a learning-by-teaching loop.

I build the training data: question–answer pairs generated from standard textbooks, each labelled by its level on Bloom's taxonomy, from recalling a fact up to creating something new.
